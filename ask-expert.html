Saddle Recommendation Questions

Will Westmoor recommend both new and used saddles?

Yes. Tell us whether you prefer new, used, or either. Recommendations are based on your needs, budget, and what we have in stock.

Do I need to answer every question?

No. Complete as much as you can. Rider measurements, riding type, budget, horse information, and clear photos are especially helpful.

Why do you need photos of my horse?

Photos help us see general back length, shoulder width, wither shape, and other characteristics that written descriptions may not capture.

Does a recommendation guarantee saddle fit?

No. The recommendation narrows the choices using the information you supply. Final fit must be evaluated on the individual horse with the intended rider and pad.

Can I trade my current saddle toward a recommended saddle?

Yes. Select “Yes” or “Possibly” in the form. An approved trade value can be applied toward a qualifying new or used saddle. See our Trade-In page.
