Our Story

Westmoor Saddlery is built on a simple belief: every rider deserves a saddle that feels as good on the 50th ride as it does on the first.

We carefully select premium Western and English saddles — new and used — that combine comfort, durability, and lasting craftsmanship. From trail and ranch work to the arena and endurance, we help riders find the right seat, tree, and fit.

Along with saddles you’ll find quality pads, boots, helmets, horse rugs, and everyday tack. We value clear product details, fair pricing, and practical advice so you can order with confidence.

Many of our customers return for a second saddle or refer friends — a trust we work hard to earn every day through clear communication, careful shipping, and a straightforward trade-in program.

Quality saddles. Honest fit. Ready to ride.

What We Stand For
