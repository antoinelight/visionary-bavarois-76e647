Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 16, 2026

Westmoor Saddlery (“we,” “us,” or “our”) respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect when you use our website and how we use it.

1. Information We Collect

  • Order information: name, shipping address, email, phone, and items ordered when you check out.
  • Contact information: details you send via our contact or newsletter forms.
  • Technical data: browser type, device, and approximate location may be collected by our host (Netlify) and analytics tools if enabled.
  • Payment data: card and PayPal payments are processed by Stripe and/or PayPal. We do not store full card numbers on our servers.

2. How We Use Information

  • To process and ship orders
  • To respond to questions and provide customer support
  • To send order updates and, if you opt in, promotional emails
  • To improve our website and prevent fraud

3. Sharing of Information

We share information only as needed with:

  • Payment processors (Stripe, PayPal)
  • Shipping carriers
  • Service providers who host email or forms (e.g. Netlify)
  • Authorities when required by law

We do not sell your personal information.

4. Cookies

We use essential cookies/local storage for your shopping cart. If we add analytics (e.g. Google Analytics), that may use additional cookies. You can control cookies in your browser settings.

5. Data Security

We use HTTPS and reputable payment providers. No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure; we take reasonable steps to protect your data.

6. Your Rights

Depending on your state (including California and other US privacy laws), you may request access, correction, or deletion of your personal information. Contact us using the details below.

7. Children

Our site is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.
