Shipping & Returns

Home / Shipping & Returns

Shipping

  • Free shipping on orders over $150 (most saddles qualify).
  • Orders under $150 ship at a flat rate (shown at checkout, typically around $15 within the contiguous US) or a calculated rate for heavier items — we will confirm before you pay.
  • How shipping is calculated: At checkout, orders $150+ show FREE shipping. Under $150, a flat shipping charge is added. Alaska, Hawaii, and international rates are quoted after you place the order.
  • Delivery time: Most in-stock items ship within 2–5 business days. Used saddles and special orders may take longer; we will confirm.
  • Saddles are carefully packed and typically ship within 1–3 business days.
  • Most domestic deliveries arrive within 3–7 business days after shipping. You will receive tracking information by email.
  • We ship from United States. Local pickup is also available by arrangement.
Customers frequently comment on packaging and speed: “The saddle was packed well and it’s beautiful.” — Robyn Lebo. We take care so your saddle arrives ready to ride.

Returns

  • We offer hassle-free returns on unused items in original condition within a reasonable window (contact us for current details).
  • To start a return, call +1 (253) 660-7454 or email support@westmoorsad.netlify.app with your order number.
  • Return shipping is the customer’s responsibility unless the item arrived damaged or incorrect.
  • Used saddles and custom/semi-custom items may have different return terms — we will confirm at purchase.

Damaged or Incorrect Orders

If your package arrives damaged or the wrong item was sent, contact us immediately with photos. We will arrange a replacement or refund as quickly as possible.

New products

Unworn/unused items in original condition may be returned within 30 days of delivery. Buyer pays return shipping unless the item is defective or not as described. Refunds are issued to the original payment method after inspection.

Used saddle returns

Used saddles are sold as described (condition notes and photos). Returns are accepted within 14 days only if the item differs materially from the listing. Saddles that have been ridden, altered, or damaged after delivery are not eligible. Contact us on WhatsApp or phone before returning any used saddle.

Free shipping

Orders over $150 ship free within the contiguous US unless otherwise noted on the product. Large items may require freight — we will confirm before finalizing.
